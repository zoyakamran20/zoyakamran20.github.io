About me

I'm diligent, curious, and most at home where careful analysis meets real people.

I grew up between Jeddah and Al Khobar in Saudi Arabia and now call Mississauga, Ontario home. Moving between cultures taught me to listen closely and explain clearly, and I bring both of those habits to my work with data.

At McMaster I studied mathematical finance, regression and data science methods. During my co-op at CIBC Mellon I put that into practice, reconciling assets and calculating NAVs where every decimal mattered. Away from the spreadsheets I've mentored fellow math students, competed in Mock Trial and supported community outreach with Indigenous communities.
